## Technique Execution: Cross-Pollination

**Focus:** Transfer solutions from consumer tech platforms to discover differentiation beyond EdTech competitors

### Ideas Generated

**[Discovery #1]**: Teacher-First Category Architecture
_Concept:_ Display ALL tools organized by consistent categories that match actual teacher workflows and needs (like "Lesson Planning," "Student Assessment," "Visual Content Creation," "Classroom Management"). Everything is visible - no algorithmic curation or hiding. Edge cases go to "Others" category for completeness without cluttering core needs.
_Novelty:_ Radical transparency combined with teacher workflow logic. Competitors use tech categories ("AI Assistants," "Generators") instead of teacher job-to-be-done categories. This is "show me tools for what I'm trying to accomplish" not "show me tools by technology type."

**[Discovery #2]**: Tech Categories vs. Teacher Job Categories
_Concept:_ Current research uses TECHNOLOGY categories ("AI assistants," "presentation tools," "flashcard generators"). But teachers don't think "I need a flashcard generator today" - they think "I need to help students review for Friday's test." Reframe categories around teacher JOBS-TO-BE-DONE instead of technology types. Organize by teacher workflow moment: "Planning Tomorrow's Lesson," "Creating Visual Materials," "Assessing Student Learning," "Communicating with Families," "Differentiating Instruction," "Professional Development."
_Novelty:_ Competitors organize by tool type. You organize by teacher workflow moment.

**[Discovery #3]**: The Universal Teacher Taxonomy
_Concept:_ Create the FIRST standardized teacher workflow taxonomy for AI tools. Every tool gets tagged with: Primary Category + Time Required + Grade Level Fit + Tech Skill Level + Privacy Compliance Status. Teachers can view "everything" but filter by their exact context. Not personalization algorithm (which hides things) but a powerful filtering system where teachers control what they see. "Show me ALL tools for creating materials that take under 15 minutes and are FERPA compliant for middle school."
_Novelty:_ Transparency + control instead of algorithmic curation. Teachers see everything but can instantly filter to their exact teaching context.

**[Research Gap Identified]**: Assessment & Feedback tools category missing from current research (e.g., NotebookLM and similar tools exist but not documented). This represents a research completeness opportunity, not a market gap.
