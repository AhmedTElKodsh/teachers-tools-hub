# 🍎 Teachers Tools Hub - User Guide

Welcome to the Teachers Tools Hub! This guide helps K-12 educators find verified, free AI tools organized by **what you need to do** (your workflow) rather than technical jargon.

## 🎯 Getting Started

### 1. The Dashboard
When you open the hub, you will see the **Dashboard**.
- **Left Sidebar:** Your navigation center.
- **Main Grid:** The collection of available tools.

### 2. Finding a Tool by Workflow
We know you don't look for "Generative AI LLMs"—you look for "Lesson Planning" help.

1.  Look at the **"Workflow Categories"** list on the left sidebar.
2.  Click on a category that matches your current task:
    *   **Lesson Planning:** For curriculum mapping, handouts, and rubrics.
    *   **Student Assessment:** For grading helpers and feedback generators.
    *   **Visual Content:** For creating slides, posters, or diagrams.
    *   (and more...)
3.  The main grid will instantly filter to show *only* the tools optimized for that task.
4.  To see everything again, click **"All Tools"** at the top of the list.

### 3. Understanding Tool Cards
Each tool card provides critical "at-a-glance" info:
- **Name & Description:** What it is.
- **Best For:** A quick tag (e.g., "Grading", "Images").
- **⚠️ Free Tier Limits:** We explicitly state what you get for free (e.g., "3 exports/day") so there are no surprises.
- **Visit Website:** A direct link to the tool.

## 💡 Pro Tips
- **Stack Your Tools:** Use a *Lesson Planning* tool to generate text, then copy it into a *Presentation Tool* to make slides. This "stacking" is how you save 7-10 hours/week!
- **Check Limitations:** Always check the "Free Tier" note before assigning a tool to students to ensure they won't hit a paywall mid-class.
